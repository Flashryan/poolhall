The build

What you're getting

A proper custom recruitment site, built around Poolhall rather than dropped on top of a template.

01Your own site, off Wix

A modern site on your own domain, designed around your brand. You own the code, the hosting and every account when it's done. No renting, no lock-in.

02Your jobs, pulled from Giig

Roles sync across from Giig onto your own pages a few times a day, with a "sync now" button when you've got something urgent. They update and expire on their own.

03Applications come straight to you

People apply on your site, CV and all. The CV lands with you by email the second they hit send, and the application's logged against the right job in Giig automatically, so you're not re-typing anything. If it ever hits a snag, it's held safely and tried again, so nothing slips through.

04Candidate logins & saved jobs

Candidates can set up an account, save the jobs they're interested in, and come back to pick up where they left off. Their details stay handy for next time they apply.

05Eligible for Google for Jobs

Every role is built so it can show up in Google's jobs box, which is the one thing the current Giig widget can't do because it sits on a no-index page.

06Featured jobs & reviews

A swipe-along featured-jobs slider on the home page, and a Google reviews slider showing off your real five-star feedback.

And the rest of it

  • Separate, clearly signposted journeys for people looking for work and businesses looking to hire
  • Full job search with filters: keyword, location, job type and salary (plus sector and remote/hybrid, depending on how your roles are tagged in Giig)
  • All the usual pages: home, jobs, single job, apply, employers, about, sectors, meet the team and contact
  • Privacy, cookie and terms pages, and a cookie banner
  • Quick on mobile, accessible, and the on-page SEO done properly
  • The move off Wix, hosting set up, launch, and a sit-down so you're comfortable running it
